1. Description of Service

Easy Flashcards is a mobile application for studying with flashcards. It lets you import a deck from a CSV or JSON file on your device or write one by hand, organise decks into categories, study them card by card, answer each card as correct, so-so or incorrect, review the cards you got wrong, and see statistics about your progress. You can also export a deck to a file, and, optionally, browse and download decks shared by other users or share your own decks with them. Apart from these community features and the optional tip, the application operates offline.

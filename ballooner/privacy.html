Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 3, 2026

Key Summary: Ballooner is an offline comic creation app. Your comics, your photos and your text stay on your device. We do not collect, store, transmit or share any personal data, and we operate no servers.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how the application Ballooner ("the App") handles your information. Ballooner lets you build short comics from pictures already on your device: you choose a panel layout, place your images, and add speech balloons. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it.

2. Data Collection and Usage

No personal data is collected or transmitted.

Everything you create works locally on your device. We do not operate remote servers or cloud databases, we do not require an account or a sign-in, and we do not use third-party analytics, advertising or tracking tools.

3. What the App Stores on Your Device

To let you come back to a comic and keep editing it, the App saves the following in its own private storage area:

  • Your comics: the panel layout, the position and framing of each image, the balloons and their text, and the name you gave the comic. These are kept in a local database on the device.
  • Copies of the pictures you choose: when you add a picture to a panel, the App copies that file into its own private folder so that the comic keeps working even if the original is later moved or deleted. These copies are not readable by other apps.
  • Your settings: such as the display language you select in the Settings screen.

None of this leaves your device unless you deliberately export or share a comic.

4. Device Permissions and Filesystem Access

The App declares no runtime permissions at all. In particular, it does not request broad access to your photo library or device storage. Instead:

  • Choosing pictures: the App opens the system photo picker provided by Android. You select exactly which pictures to use, and the App receives only those. It never browses or scans your gallery.
  • Exporting a comic: when you export a comic as a PNG, Android asks you where to save it. The App can only write to the single location you choose.
  • Sharing a comic: when you share a comic, the App renders a PNG into its own temporary area and hands that single file to the app you pick from the Android share sheet. What that other app does with the image is governed by that app's own privacy policy.

The App never deletes, moves or modifies your original photos.

5. Network Access and Third-Party Services

The App contains no advertising, profiling, behavioural analytics or tracking SDKs (no Google Analytics, no AdMob, no Facebook SDK, or similar). It has no crash reporting service and sends no telemetry.

For transparency: the comic lettering uses Android's standard downloadable fonts mechanism. Font files may therefore be fetched and cached by the operating system's font provider on your device. That request is handled by Android itself, carries no information about you or your comics, and the App receives nothing in return but the typeface.

6. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children. Since no data is collected or transmitted, no profile of any user is ever created.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Because the App stores no user data on remote servers, there is no account to delete. You can delete any individual comic from inside the App at any time. Uninstalling the application permanently removes its local database, its imported image copies and its saved settings from the device. Comics you have already exported or shared remain wherever you saved or sent them.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The revised version will be published with a new "Last Updated" date.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, you can contact the developer via the email address listed on the app's store page.

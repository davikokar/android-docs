Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 8, 2026

Key Summary: Easy Gallery is a completely offline application. It does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data or media files with external servers or third parties.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy outlines how the application Easy Gallery ("the App") handles user information and device permissions. We strictly respect user privacy and are committed to protecting it.

2. Data Collection and Usage

No personal data is collected or transmitted.

The App operates entirely locally on your mobile device. We do not operate remote servers or cloud databases, nor do we utilize third-party analytics or tracking tools.

3. Device Permissions and Filesystem Access

To deliver its core functionality as a photo gallery app, the App requests permission to access your device storage/photo library. Specifically:

  • Reading Media Files: Required to scan and display photos and images stored on your device.
  • Managing Files and Folders: Required to allow you to create new folders and organize your photos.
  • Deleting Files: Required exclusively when you manually select and initiate a command to delete one or more photos.

All actions listed above are executed locally on the user's device and under direct user control.

4. Third-Party SDKs and Services

The App does not integrate any third-party SDKs for advertising, user profiling, behavioral analytics, or tracking (e.g., no Google Analytics, AdMob, Facebook SDK, etc.).

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Because the App does not store user data on remote servers, there is no remote account deletion required. Uninstalling the application from your device will permanently remove any locally stored preferences or settings.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, you can contact the developer via the email address listed on the app's store page.

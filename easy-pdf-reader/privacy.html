Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 3, 2026

Key Summary: Easy PDF Reader is a completely offline application. It does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data or document content with external servers or third parties. Your PDF files never leave your device.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy outlines how the application Easy PDF Reader ("the App") handles user information and device access. We strictly respect user privacy and are committed to protecting it.

2. Data Collection and Usage

No personal data is collected or transmitted.

The App operates entirely locally on your mobile device. We do not operate remote servers or cloud databases, nor do we utilize third-party analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, or tracking tools.

The content of the PDF documents you open is rendered locally on your device and is never uploaded, indexed, or transmitted anywhere.

3. Device Access and File Handling

The App does not request broad storage permissions. It accesses documents only through the standard Android system mechanisms, under your explicit control:

  • System Document Picker: A PDF file is opened only when you personally select it in the Android file picker. The App receives access to that single file and nothing else.
  • "Open with" Intents: The App can be chosen to open a PDF shared from another application (for example a file manager, email client, or browser). Access is limited to the document you chose to open.
  • Persisted Access: Android may grant the App continued read access to a document you previously opened, so it can be reopened conveniently. This reference is stored locally on your device only and can be removed by clearing the App's data or uninstalling it.

The App does not scan, index, or browse your device storage, and it does not modify or delete your files.

4. Locally Stored Settings

The App stores a small number of preferences locally on your device (for example, your selected display language). These settings remain on your device, are not linked to any identity, and are removed when the App is uninstalled.

5. Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate any third-party SDKs for advertising, user profiling, behavioral analytics, or tracking (e.g., no Google Analytics, AdMob, Facebook SDK, etc.).

The App includes an optional, voluntary "tip the developer" in-app purchase. If, and only if, you choose to make such a purchase, the transaction is processed entirely by Google Play Billing. Payment and billing details are handled by Google under Google's own privacy policy; the developer never receives or stores your payment information.

6. Network Access

The App does not require an internet connection to view your documents and performs no background network communication. Any network activity is limited to Google Play's own services when an optional in-app purchase is initiated by you.

7. Children's Privacy

The App does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Because the App does not store user data on remote servers, there is no remote account deletion required. Uninstalling the application from your device will permanently remove any locally stored preferences, settings, and document references.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will become effective immediately upon publishing the updated text on this page.

10. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, you can contact the developer via the email address listed on the app's store page.

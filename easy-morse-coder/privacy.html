Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 3, 2026

Key Summary: Easy Morse Coding is a completely offline application. It does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. The text you type is converted to Morse code entirely on your device and is never sent anywhere.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy outlines how the application Easy Morse Coding ("the App") handles user information and device permissions. We strictly respect user privacy and are committed to protecting it.

2. Data Collection and Usage

No personal data is collected or transmitted.

The App operates entirely locally on your mobile device. We do not operate remote servers or cloud databases, nor do we utilize third-party analytics or tracking tools.

Any message you enter for encoding is processed in memory on your device for the sole purpose of generating and playing back the corresponding Morse code signal. Messages are not uploaded, logged, or shared.

3. Device Permissions and Hardware Access

To deliver its core functionality as a Morse code signalling app, the App requests the following permissions and uses the following device hardware:

  • Camera Permission (Flashlight): Required exclusively to switch the device's camera flash LED on and off in order to transmit Morse code as light signals. The App does not capture, record, preview, or store photos or video of any kind.
  • Audio Output: Used to generate a tone through the device speaker when the sound output option is enabled. The App does not use or request access to the microphone.

All actions listed above are executed locally on the user's device and under direct user control. The flashlight and sound outputs are only activated when you start playback.

4. Locally Stored Settings

The App stores a small set of preferences locally on your device, such as your selected display language and your playback settings (timing, countdown duration, repeat, and output options). These preferences never leave your device and contain no personally identifiable information.

5. In-App Purchases

The App may offer an optional, voluntary one-time "support the developer" purchase. Such purchases are processed entirely by Google Play Billing. We never receive, see, or store your payment card details, billing address, or any other financial information. The App only receives a confirmation from Google Play that a purchase has completed. Please refer to Google's Privacy Policy for details on how Google processes payment data.

6. Third-Party SDKs and Services

Apart from the Google Play Billing library required to process the optional support purchase, the App does not integrate any third-party SDKs for advertising, user profiling, behavioral analytics, or tracking (e.g., no Google Analytics, AdMob, Facebook SDK, etc.).

7. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children. Because no data is collected or transmitted, the App is safe to use at any age.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Because the App does not store user data on remote servers, there is no remote account deletion required. Uninstalling the application from your device will permanently remove any locally stored preferences or settings.

9. Changes to This Policy

We reserve the right to update this Privacy Policy. Any changes will become effective immediately upon publishing the updated text, and the "Last Updated" date above will be revised accordingly.

10. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, you can contact the developer via the email address listed on the app's store page.
